About

The short version

I'm the son of a small business owner, and I've spent my career proving how much is possible at the community level. I trained and managed over 1,000 college students as they launched their first profitable businesses — putting more than $10 million in their pockets to pay for school.

Since then I've built across the table: an MBA, a VC-backed startup, a small business of my own, and a real estate portfolio — in every one of the fifty states.
